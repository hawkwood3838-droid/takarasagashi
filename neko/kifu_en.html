About donations

Part of the money this game receives goes to cat rescue groups.

What becomes a donation

  • Gifts (¥120 / ¥240 / ¥480): half of the price
  • Donate: the full amount minus about 7.2% in payment and transfer fees
  • Bring them home (¥500): a portion of the proceeds

Where it goes

Payments made from the Japanese version go to a Japanese cat rescue group (CATS WELCARE, a certified non-profit in Osaka). Payments made from the English version go to Alley Cat Allies (USA), a cat-only organization working for community cats through trap-neuter-return and advocacy.
