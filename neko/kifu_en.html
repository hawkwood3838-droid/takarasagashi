About our donations

The creator of this game donates part of the revenue from in-game items to cat rescue groups. This page does not accept donations.

How much of the revenue is donated

  • Gifts (¥120 / ¥240 / ¥480): half of the revenue
  • Bring them home (¥500): a portion of the revenue

Where it goes

Revenue from the Japanese version goes to a Japanese cat rescue group (CATS WELCARE, a certified non-profit in Osaka). Revenue from all other language versions goes to Alley Cat Allies (USA), a cat-only organization working for community cats through trap-neuter-return and advocacy.
